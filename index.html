About me

I'm Diana, a FIT CTU student from Slovakia trying to make my way in the world of web development. During the past few years, solving complex problems became my motivation, as well as my everyday duty. I enjoy both the logical and creative aspects of development, programming itself or other parts of the IT world, and I'm always excited to learn something new.

Before FIT, I spent a year studying business at Avans University of Applied Sciences in the Netherlands. This experience taught me a lot about the economics and management, as well as leadership, teamwork and communication with people from different backgrounds. Although I enjoyed my time there, I quickly realised my passion lies elsewhere, and so I followed it to FIT.

Outside of code, I was a vice president of a Junior Achievement student company called SmellMe, I've been tutoring English since 2021 and have done part-time jobs that varied from lifeguarding to working as a cashier in the USA.
